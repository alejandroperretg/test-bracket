Three independent volumes that agree

The same quantity, computed three ways: by hand from the parameters, by the CAD kernel from the exact solid, and by trimesh from the exported triangles. If the model, the export or my maths were wrong, these would disagree.

Hand calculation

The fillet term is the interesting one. A concave fillet fills the square corner region R×R minus a quarter disc of radius R, so it adds (1 − π/4)·R² of cross-section, about 21 % of the square.

        Pipeline · one command per stage

        How the part is made

        1. ParametersSix numbers in bracket.py define the whole part.
        2. Solidbuild123d unions two boxes, fillets the inner edge and cuts two holes on the OpenCascade kernel.
        3. ExportSTEP keeps the exact geometry for CAD. STL is the triangle mesh for the slicer.
        4. Verifytrimesh and pytest check volume, watertightness, hole count and envelope.
        5. Publishmake_site.py projects the drawing views and packs the mesh for this page.
